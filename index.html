How my RAG apps answer a question

  1. 1ExtractPull the text out of PDF, TXT and DOCX files.
  2. 2Chunk and embedSplit it into pieces and store vector embeddings in ChromaDB.
  3. 3SearchFind the passages closest in meaning to the question.
  4. 4AnswerSend them to the OpenAI API for a grounded reply.
